01 · The astronomical foundation

Locally bundled Astronomy Engine 2.1.19 calculates geocentric lunar longitude. We subtract an approximate Lahiri ayanamsa (23.8531° at J2000, advancing 50.29 arcseconds/year). This is not a Swiss Ephemeris implementation; boundary results should be independently verified.

02 · The 120-year cycle

The Moon's position within its 13⅓° nakshatra sets the remaining birth dasha. Nine lords follow in order, with subperiods proportional to their years in the 120-year cycle. This site uses 365.25636 days/year. Dates display in UTC.

03 · A thoughtful interpretation

The reading combines dasha lords, whole-sign ownership, sign dignity, sign-based graha aspects, associations, dispositors, and supporting Jupiter/Saturn transit snapshots. Astrology is a cultural interpretive practice, not a scientifically validated way to predict events. Use these readings for reflection.

Birth location selects the historical time zone. Coordinates enable an approximate sidereal ascendant and whole-sign houses; Moon-based readings remain available without coordinates. Supported birth years: 1900–today. No birth details are sent to an external calculation service.
